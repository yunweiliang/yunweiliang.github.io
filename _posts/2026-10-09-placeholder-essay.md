## Why write essays

An essay is a way to think something through in public. Writing forces a vague idea into sentences, and sentences can be checked.[^checked] The point of a short placeholder like this one is only to show how paragraphs, headings, and notes sit on the page.

## How this page is built

Each essay is a single Markdown file. The title, subtitle, and date come from the front matter at the top of the file. Every `##` heading becomes a section and is listed in the table of contents automatically.

Footnotes are written in Markdown too.[^markdown] They are numbered in order, collected at the end, and each one links back to the place it was cited.
